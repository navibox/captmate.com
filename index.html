Docking mode靠泊模式

Stop to the metre精确到每一米

One tap on the ship's wheel and the screen turns into a docking display built for the last cable.

轻点舵轮按钮，屏幕即切换为专为最后一链距离设计的靠泊显示。

  • BOW / AFT sway panelSideways speed of bow and stern in knots, green to starboard, red to port. Drag the panel anywhere.
    船首 / 船尾横移面板以节为单位显示船首和船尾的横向速度，绿色向右舷、红色向左舷，面板可随意拖动。
  • Motion predictionRed hull outlines show where the ship will be in 30 seconds (adjustable), with the pivot point while turning.
    运动预测红色船体轮廓显示 30 秒（可调）后船舶所在位置，转向时同时标出转心位置。
  • Hear the rate of turnROT bar on top, plus an optional click sound that speeds up as the turn grows — eyes stay on the quay.
    “听见”转向率顶部 ROT 条形图，另有可选的提示音，转得越快滴答越密，眼睛可以一直看着码头。
  • Distance logTap once and count the metres moved along the hull line — ahead in green, astern in red.
    距离计点一下开始计算沿船体首尾线移动的米数，前进为绿色，后退为红色。

Navigation mode航行模式

Plan the passage, keep the time规划航线，准点抵达

Load a route and the monitor does the arithmetic: distance, time to go, ETA and the speed you need.

载入航线后，航线监视器自动算好剩余距离、剩余时间、ETA 以及所需航速。

  • GPX, RTZ and CSV routesImport, save and switch routes. NEXT / DEST tabs with DTG, TTG, BRG, XTE and ETA.
    支持 GPX、RTZ、CSV 航线导入、保存、切换航线。NEXT / DEST 两个标签页显示 DTG、TTG、BRG、XTE 和 ETA。
  • Target ETA → required speedSet your arrival time in any time zone; the required speed updates twice a second.
    目标 ETA → 所需航速按任意时区设定到达时间，所需航速每 0.5 秒更新一次。
  • Weather along the routeWind, waves and currents forecast for where you will be, when you will be there — and an ETA* that includes the currents.
    沿途天气预报你将在何时到达何处时的风、浪、流，并给出计入海流影响的 ETA*。

Record & replay记录与回放

Replay any voyage任意航次，随时回放

Record every NMEA and AIS line with a time stamp, then play it back at up to ten times speed — for debriefs, training, or testing at the dock.

为每一条 NMEA 和 AIS 语句加上时间戳记录下来，之后最高以 10 倍速回放，适合复盘、培训或在码头上测试。

  • Share, import, repeatExport logs, import plain NMEA files, and add any log as a replay source that loops.
    分享、导入、循环导出日志、导入普通 NMEA 文本，并可把任意日志作为可循环的回放数据源。
Everything on board全面功能

Built by mariners, for the watch为值班驾驶员打造

Native Kotlin, no third-party libraries, works offline with your own charts.

原生 Kotlin 开发、不依赖第三方库，配合离线海图无网也能用。

AIS targetsAIS 目标

Every ship around you with vectors, name, MMSI, size, destination and CPA / TCPA. Names are remembered between runs.

周围每一艘船的矢量线、船名、MMSI、尺寸、目的港以及 CPA / TCPA，船名会在下次启动时保留。

Layered charts多层海图

OpenStreetMap, Open Waters Seascape & Seamap, OpenSeaMap seamarks, MapTiler, and your own MBTiles — including S-57 ENC converted with depth areas, soundings and lights.

OpenStreetMap、Open Waters 海底地形与海图、OpenSeaMap 航标、MapTiler，以及自有 MBTiles，包括转换后的 S-57 电子海图（水深区、水深点、灯标）。

AUTO source scan一键自动扫描

Join the Pilot Plug or NMEA Wi-Fi and tap Scan. CaptMate tries the common gateways and recommends the source with the best data.

连上引航插头或 NMEA Wi-Fi 后点“扫描”，CaptMate 会尝试常见网关地址并推荐数据质量最好的数据源。

Data quality数据质量控制

Spike rejection and smoothing, latency compensation, and a per-value choice of source — NMEA, AIS or calculated.

野值剔除与平滑、延迟补偿，并可为每项数据单独选择来源：NMEA、AIS 或计算值。

Weather layers气象图层

Wind barbs, isobars, waves and currents from Open-Meteo, with a forecast time slider in three-hour steps.

来自 Open-Meteo 的风羽、等压线、海浪和海流，配有以 3 小时为步长的预报时间滑块。

Day & night themes日间 / 夜间主题

Switch themes in place without losing your view — a dark bridge at night, high contrast in the sun.

原地切换主题，不丢失当前视图：夜间不刺眼，阳光下高对比度。
