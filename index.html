CaptMate

Docking and passage navigation on your Android phone — NMEA 0183 and AIS from several sources at once, layered charts, motion prediction and route ETA.

Docking mode

ROT bar, BOW / AFT sway panel, red motion-prediction outlines, pivot point and a distance log along the hull line.

Navigation mode

Import GPX, RTZ or CSV routes. Next waypoint, BRG, XTE, DTG, TTG, ETA and the required speed for a target ETA.

AIS

Targets with vectors, names, voyage data and CPA / TCPA. Own-ship size and antenna position from AIS static data.

Charts

OpenStreetMap, Open Waters Seascape / Seamap, OpenSeaMap seamarks, MapTiler and your own MBTiles (raster or vector, S-57 converted).

Data sources

TCP, UDP, Bluetooth SPP and log replay, with an AUTO scan for Pilot Plugs and NMEA Wi-Fi gateways.

Weather

Wind, waves, currents and isobars with a forecast slider, route weather and an ETA that includes forecast currents.

Assistive tool only — not for primary navigation or collision avoidance.
